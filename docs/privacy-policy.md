# Fotolark Privacy Policy

Effective: (set on publication)

Fotolark does not collect, store, sell or share any personal information.

- **No account, no tracking, no analytics.** Fotolark does not contact any server.
- **Your photos stay on your Mac.** Fotolark works on the folders you choose and, if you
  allow it, your Photos library. Face grouping, duplicate finding and reading text in
  photos are done on your Mac with Apple's built-in Vision framework. The results are kept
  only on your Mac.
- **Your edits** are saved in small files next to your photos, or inside Fotolark's own
  storage on your Mac if you choose that in Settings.
- **Photos library access** is asked for only when you open Apple Photos in Fotolark, and
  you can turn it off at any time in System Settings › Privacy & Security › Photos.
