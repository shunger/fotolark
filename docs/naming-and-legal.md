# Naming and legal notes

As of Nov 6, 2026. This is a first screen from web searches and DNS lookups, not legal clearance. Before launch, confirm on the official USPTO trademark search (https://tmsearch.uspto.gov, class 9 software), and consider a short review with an IP lawyer if Fotolark will be sold.

## Decision

**Name: Fotolark.** No apps or trademarks found under that name; fotolark.com (whois: "No match") and fotolark.app (no DNS) both appeared unregistered.

## Rules for staying clear of Picasa / Google

- Copyright covers code, artwork, icons and text, not features. Write all code fresh; copy nothing.
- Never use "Picasa", "Picasso" or Google phrases (e.g. "I'm feeling lucky") in the app. The auto-fix button is "Magic fix".
- Own icon, colors, button graphics and layout. A sidebar + grid is fine; a pixel-for-pixel copy of Picasa's screens is not.
- Marketing may say "for people who miss Picasa" as a factual mention, never implying a link to Google.
- Picasa launched in 2002; patents from that era have mostly expired. Image processing relies on Apple frameworks.
