## Description

Fotolark shows the photos already in your folders and makes them better in seconds. Your
originals are never changed: every edit can be undone, and edits travel with your photos
in small files beside them.

FIX
• Magic fix, auto contrast and auto color in one click
• Crop to print sizes, straighten, rotate, resize
• Light, color, curves, levels, noise reduction, sharpening
• Remove blemishes and unwanted objects, fix red eye, soften skin
• Brush and gradient adjustments, background blur, lens correction
• Looks, tilt-shift, borders, captions, arrows and watermarks

ORGANIZE
• Star ratings, pick and reject, tags and albums across folders
• Search by name, place in your folders, date, camera and the text in your photos
• People: faces grouped on your Mac, ready to name
• Find identical and look-alike duplicates

IMPORT, SHARE AND PRINT
• Import from iPhone, cameras, memory cards, drives and Apple Photos
• Bulk convert, resize, rename and fix hundreds of photos at once
• Export, share, print standard sizes, contact sheets, PDFs, collages and slideshows
• Shortcuts actions, Finder Services and watch folders

PRIVATE
No account. No tracking. Face grouping, duplicate finding and text recognition happen on
your Mac, and nothing is sent anywhere.
